# Pricing

What each library costs, from each vendor's own pricing page, with the date it was read. Prices change — the
source link is the authority; this table is a dated copy. Taxes, discounts negotiated with sales, and renewal
prices are not included.

[pricing.csv](pricing.csv) has every published licence. Below, four common situations, each priced with the
cheapest published licence that covers it **as we read the vendor's published terms** — read the vendor's
licence agreement before buying; where a term is ambiguous it is noted.

| Situation | SelectPdf | IronPDF |
|---|---|---|
| 1 developer, an internal application on 1 server | Single Developer — **$499** | Lite — **$999** |
| 1 developer, a SaaS product or software sold to customers | Single Developer OEM — **$799** | Lite + royalty-free OEM redistribution add-on — **$2,998** |
| 5 developers, up to 10 servers, internal | 5-Developers — **$799** | Professional (Plus covers 3 developers) — **$2,399** |
| Unlimited developers, SaaS or redistributed | Enterprise OEM — **$1,599** | Unlimited + OEM add-on — **$6,798** |

Notes, from the pricing pages (2026-10-07):

- **Both** sell perpetual licences with one year of updates and support included; after that, renewal is
  optional (SelectPdf: maintenance plans; IronPDF: support and updates paid per year).
- **SelectPdf** licences count developers and deployment machines; OEM tiers remove the machine limit and
  allow shipping inside your product. One licence covers SelectPdf.Universal and the classic Windows library.
- **IronPDF** licences count developers, "locations" (described as developer locations by city) and projects.
  The royalty-free OEM redistribution add-on is described as "for building commercial software and paid SaaS
  services"; its price is shown as $1,999 "now" against $3,999 "later". The Professional and Unlimited prices
  are shown as discounts from $2,999 and $5,999.
- **Free options:** SelectPdf's trial is the full library with a watermark on every page, and the Community
  Edition (the HTML to PDF converter only) is free, including in production, up to 5 pages per PDF. IronPDF's trial key unlocks full functionality
  for 30 days, for development and testing.
